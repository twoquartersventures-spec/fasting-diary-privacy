Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Information We Collect

This app does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous usage data and crash reports to improve stability and performance. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Data Storage

All fasting timer data is stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate any backend server that stores user data.

Foreground Service

The app uses a foreground service to maintain a persistent timer notification and ensure reliable alarm functionality.

Data Sharing

No user data is sold or shared with third parties.
